# Member Stock Holding Limit

Under the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act in South Africa, an adult is permitted to cultivate a maximum of four (4) flowering cannabis plants in a private place. 

Key details regarding private cultivation:

    Per Adult/Household: The limit is generally four flowering plants per adult, or up to eight plants per household if more than one adult lives there. Some interpretations suggest a maximum of 600g of dried cannabis per person or 1.2kg per household.
    Private Space Definition: Plants must be grown in a private space (e.g., backyard, balcony, indoors) that is not visible or accessible to the public or minors.
    Seedlings: There is no legal limit to the number of cannabis seeds or seedlings a private user may possess.
    No Commercial Use: Plants are strictly for personal use. Selling cannabis or cannabis products remains illegal.
    Shared Accommodation: If two or more adults share a household, they are permitted up to 1.2 kilograms of dried cannabis (or 1,200 grams).
    Prohibition on Public Use: While you can grow it, you cannot smoke or consume it in public or in the presence of non-consenting adults/minors.

System needs to prevent members from owning more than 4 flowering plants at any 1 time. To prevent this, members can be prompted to swap a flowering plant for a seedling to reduce stock-holding.
