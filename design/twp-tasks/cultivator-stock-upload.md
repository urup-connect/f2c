# Cultivator Stock Upload

Cultivators can load individual plants or batch upload multiple plants using an excel template.

When entering or uploading plant info the following is required:
* Cultivator ID,
* Cultivator plant ID,
* Strain,
* Grow Price,
* Planting Date,
* Estimated Bloom Date,
* Estimated Harvest Date,
* Minimum Yield,
* Finished Product Types Available. 
* Optional: Crop / batch Number.

System generated/calculated fields:
* Plant serial,
* Cultivator Pseudonym,
* Leaf Rating,
* Status,
* Days to Harvest,
* Days to Bloom.
