# Member Plant Purchase

When shopping for plants, members will first browse available strains:

Strains should have a generic listing page with general strain info and lowest available grow price (Grow Price From).

Member can select the strain and then see which cultivators offer the strain with prices, average star-ratings, cultivators short description for the strain, minimum yield, and available finished product types.

Member then selects the cultivator and can then see available plants - planting/harvest dates with number of plants per date. (Not necessary to see each serialised plant)

Member chooses the planting/harvest date and can add a quantity of plants to cart. System will allocate specific plants (serial numbers) to the member.
