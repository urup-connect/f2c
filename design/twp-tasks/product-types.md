# Finished Product Types
Initially 2 types will be available for all plants: pre-rolls & loose cannabis. Other types may become available later.

The finished product type must be chosen when the plant is harvested. At harvest the member must be notifies to finalise the transaction by choosing type and delivery address.

Pre-rolls and loose will not have a cost to start with, so nothing is due to be paid when the members makes this choice.

When other types come online, it may require the member to pay something based on their choice as processing will have extra costs.
